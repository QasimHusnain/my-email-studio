# Email Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/`: how the business looks and sounds, such as its voice and style.
- `leads/`: the lists of people campaigns are written for.
- `campaigns/`: the plans and emails for each campaign.
- `notes/`: working notes, ideas and research.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people.
  - Real people includes Brightwell Heating's customers and anyone on a real customer list.
  - Sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in @resend.dev) and to my own email address.
  - A guard will enforce this from the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
