# Campaign plan: Boiler service reminder

Brightwell Heating Ltd, Bradford, West Yorkshire.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Name:** Warm Home Winter Check
- **Summary:** A short, friendly email series asking past customers and enquirers to book their yearly boiler service with Daniel before the coldest weeks of January and February.
- **Main goal:** 100 boiler service or CP12 bookings from the email list between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Fill the diary for January with booked services, so breakdown calls are fewer when the weather is worst.
  - Get landlords' CP12 dates on record so next year's reminder can go out at the right time for each property.
  - Ask happy customers for a Google review after their service.

**Is 100 realistic?** 100 bookings from about 1,000 contacts is a 10% booking rate. That is high for email, but this is a warm list of people who already know the business, and landlords must renew a CP12 every year by law. It is a stretch target, not a safe one. Section 7 sets weekly checkpoints so we know early if we are behind.

## 2. Target audience

About 1,000 past customers and enquirers across Bradford and West Yorkshire. Group sizes are not yet known; count them in `leads/` before writing (see Next steps).

| Group | Who they are | What worries them | What moves them |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Families with young children** | Parents who need hot water and a warm house every day | Waking to a cold house with little ones, no hot water for baths, waiting days for an engineer or parts in January | Peace of mind, a quick and easy booking, a local engineer they can trust in the home |
| **Homeowners with older relatives at home** | Adults caring for a parent or grandparent who feels the cold | A loved one being cold if the boiler stops, and the boiler running safely | Reassurance, a patient engineer, a yearly check by a Gas Safe engineer |
| **Landlords (CP12)** | Landlords with one or more rented homes | Keeping on top of the yearly gas safety certificate, tenant complaints, winter breakdowns in a rental | Getting the certificate and service done in one visit, a written price before work starts, a reliable local engineer |

**Buying stage:** All three groups already know the business, so this is a **decision-stage** (retention) campaign. The job is to prompt action, not to introduce the company.

**Where they are:** Their inbox, plus phone and WhatsApp. Email is the only paid-for-nothing channel we are using; phone and WhatsApp are how many will reply.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Book your yearly boiler service now with Daniel, your local Gas Safe registered engineer, so your home stays warm through the coldest weeks.

| Supporting message | Proof point (from `brand/voice.md`) |
|---|---|
| A winter breakdown is a real hassle: a cold house, no hot water, and a wait for an engineer or parts in January. A service now is the easy fix. | Honest picture only; no scare words. 10+ years' experience, 500+ jobs. |
| You'll deal with Daniel, a local engineer, not a call centre. | Daniel by name; Gas Safe registration No. 000000 with a link to check it. |
| You'll know the price before any work starts. | A written price agreed before any work starts. No price figures in emails. |
| Local people trust us. | One approved review, word for word, with the first name. 5-star rated on Google, from 75+ reviews (check it is still 5.0 before each send). |

**Message by group:**

- **Families:** warm house and hot water for the children; a quick, tidy visit.
- **Older relatives:** keeping a loved one warm; one calm line that a yearly check by a Gas Safe engineer keeps the boiler running safely.
- **Landlords:** CP12 and service in one visit; a written price; less chance of a winter call-out from a tenant.

**Suggested review per group** (copied word for word, never edited):

- Families: "Five star service from Daniel. Turned up when he said he would, explained everything clearly and left the place tidy. Thank you." – Sarah
- Older relatives: "Brilliant work by Daniel and the team." – Priya
- Landlords: "Great service from Brightwell Heating. Friendly, on time and the price was exactly as quoted. Would use again." – Tom

**Must not appear in any email** (from the brand voice): pressure words or countdowns, price figures or discounts, same-day or 24/7 promises, scare words, unexplained trade jargon, accreditations we don't hold, or warranty claims beyond the approved wording.

## 4. Channel strategy

| Channel | Why it fits | Format | Effort | Budget |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Email** (main) | We already have the list, it costs almost nothing, and the audience already knows us | Short plain-text-style emails, one action each, three versions per send (one per group) | Low to medium | Most of the small budget goes on the email tool and Daniel's time |
| **Phone and WhatsApp** (response) | Many local customers would rather ring or message than fill in a form | 07700900123 beside every "Book your service" button | Low, but needs someone to answer within opening hours | None |
| **Website contact page** (response) | Where the "Book your service" button goes | Existing page: https://brightwellheating.example/contact/ | Low; check it works on phones | None |

No paid ads, social posts or printed material in this campaign, as agreed.

## 5. Content calendar

Campaign weeks run Monday to Sunday. Sends go out on Tuesday mornings, when inboxes are quieter than Monday. Each send goes in three versions, one per group. Later emails go only to people who have not booked yet.

| 1 | 7 – 13 Dec | **Email 1: "Is your boiler ready for winter?"** Main reminder | Email, all three groups (families, older relatives, landlords) | Send Tue 8 Dec | Not started |
| 2 | 14 – 20 Dec | **Email 2: Friendly follow-up** with a review and the Gas Safe check link | Email, all three groups (families, older relatives, landlords), not yet booked only | Send Tue 15 Dec. Checkpoint: 50 bookings | Not started |
| 3 | 21 – 27 Dec | **No send.** Christmas week (Fri 25, Sat 26 Dec) | — | Answer replies and calls; log bookings | — |

**Dependencies:**

- Bookings must be logged daily, so Emails 2 and 3 skip people who have already booked.

## 7. Success metrics

**Main KPI:** 100 bookings (boiler service or CP12) from people on the list, 7 December 2026 to 3 January 2027.

| Checkpoint | Target |
|---|---|
| End of week 1 (13 Dec) | 25 bookings |
| End of week 2 (20 Dec) | 50 bookings |
| End of week 3 (27 Dec) | 65 bookings |
| End of week 4 (3 Jan) | 100 bookings |

**Secondary KPIs:**

| KPI | Target | How we track it |
|---|---|---|
| Open rate | 40%+ | Email tool report |
| Click rate on "Book your service" | 5%+ | Email tool report; add `?utm_source=email&utm_campaign=winter-check` to the contact link |
| Bookings by route | Record all | Booking log: form, phone, WhatsApp |
| Bookings by group | Record all | Booking log; shows which message worked best |
| Unsubscribes | Under 1% per send | Email tool report |
| New Google reviews | 10+ | Count on the Google listing before and after |

**Reporting:** Count bookings every Monday morning against the checkpoint. Full report after 3 January.

## 9. Risks and mitigations

| Risk | What could happen | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Christmas timing** | Weeks 3 and 4 fall over Christmas and New Year, when people are busy and away | No send in Christmas week; Email 3 goes before New Year's Day; front-load effort into weeks 1 and 2 |
| **Behind target** | Fewer than 50 bookings by 20 December | Look at which group is booking least and rewrite their Email 3; consider a short personal follow-up call to landlords whose CP12 is due |
| **Too many bookings at once** | More requests than Daniel can fit in, leading to long waits | Agree diary capacity in prep; never promise same-day; offer the next available date honestly |
| **Brand slip** | An email uses pressure words, a price, or a reworded review | Run every email through a brand review against `brand/voice.md` before sign-off |
| **Sending to the wrong people** | A test email reaches real customers | Test only in practice mode to our own address; the real list is never used in testing |
