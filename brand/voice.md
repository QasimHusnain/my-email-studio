# Brand voice: Brightwell Heating

Brightwell Heating Ltd is a local Gas Safe registered heating company in Bradford, West Yorkshire, led by engineer Daniel Hart. It installs, services and repairs boilers and central heating, and does landlord gas safety certificates (CP12). We write to homeowners and families across Bradford and West Yorkshire, many with young children or older relatives at home. Our first campaign reminds them to book their annual boiler service before the coldest weeks.

## Our voice in three words

Local, honest, reassuring.

Write like a trusted local engineer talking to a neighbour: plain English, patient and straight-talking. Never pushy, never salesy. We can paint an honest picture of a winter breakdown (waking to a cold house, no hot water, waiting for an engineer or parts in January), then offer the easy fix calmly. One calm line on safety is fine: a yearly check by a Gas Safe engineer keeps the boiler running safely.

## Words we use

- Gas Safe registered
- local
- written quote
- plain English

## Words we avoid

- Pressure: hurry, act now, don't miss out, limited time, countdowns, "only X slots left"
- Price-cutting: cheapest, deal, bargain, discount
- Overpromises: 24/7, guaranteed same-day, instant
- Scare words: deadly, killer, silent killer, explosion
- Trade jargon without a plain-English explanation, such as "heat exchanger" or "flue gas analysis"

## How we build trust

Trust is why customers call us, so every email uses facts the reader can check:

- Daniel by name, as a local engineer, not a call centre.
- Gas Safe registration No. 000000, with a link to check it on the Gas Safe Register.
- A written price agreed before any work starts.
- One short customer review, copied word for word from the approved list below, with the reviewer's first name (for example "– Sarah"). Never invent, reword, shorten or combine reviews, and never add an area the review doesn't give.

## Claims we must never make

- No fixed prices or price figures. Prices are agreed in a written quote.
- No promise of same-day visits. Same-day may be possible, depending on workload and location.
- No 24/7 or emergency-anytime claims. Opening hours are Mon–Fri 07:30–20:00, Sat 08:00–20:00, Sun 08:30–20:00.
- No warranty promises beyond "up to 10-year manufacturer warranties, depending on manufacturer, model and annual servicing".
- No accreditations we don't hold, such as TrustMark or Which? Trusted Trader.
- No carbon monoxide scare stories or claims that a boiler is dangerous.
